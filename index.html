Logos Bookshelf

Unofficial personal project, not affiliated with Logos or Faithlife. A click opens the book in Logos (you need to be signed in). Shift+click, right-click, or press i on a focused book for details; press p to pin it to the chosen shelf. Thickness follows the page count of the exported copy; a dotted top edge marks an excerpt (first 100 pages); a gold ribbon marks a pinned book; a number in the corner counts copies or passage references. Notes and shelves stay in your browser. Source.
